📹 Submit Content

            
            
            

                

                    POST
                    /api/submissions/auto
                

                

                    Submit content for tracking. Platform is automatically detected from URL. Requires a verified account matching the content author.
                

                

                    
                    
                

                

                    Detected platform: 
                

                

                    ✨ Auto-detect: Just paste the URL - we'll detect YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram automatically!
                

                

            


            
            

                

                    POST
                    /api/submissions/:platform/batch
                

                

                    Submit multiple content URLs at once (up to 50). More efficient for bulk submissions.
                

                

                    
                    

                        
                        
                    

                

                

                    💡 Tip: Batch submission processes 5 URLs at a time to avoid rate limits. Same total cost as individual submissions!
